Level 3 data for B2B payments

If you sell to other businesses, sending Level 3 (line-item) data can qualify some transactions for lower interchange. Since April 18, 2026, Visa's lowest rates on corporate, purchasing and fleet cards require verified Level 3 data. On small-business cards, it mainly helps card-not-present and invoiced payments.

Not for everyone: on small-business cards paid in person, Level 3 doesn't lower the rate at all. We'll tell you whether it fits how your customers actually pay.

Dual pricing

Dual pricing shows two prices, a cash price and a card price, both posted clearly so customers see them before they pay. It's different from adding a fee at checkout.

State rules vary, and card network display rules apply. We'll go over what applies where you do business before you set it up.
